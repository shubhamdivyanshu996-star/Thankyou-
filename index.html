Thank you, Dora. 💗

Dear Divya,

I don't think a simple thank-you could ever explain how grateful I am that you entered my life. Still, I want to try.

Thank you for listening to my endless bakwaas, for laughing with me, for being patient with me, and for making ordinary conversations feel special.

Thank you for being a safe place in a world that sometimes feels too loud. For the moments when I can talk without having every word figured out, and for making me feel like I don't always have to hide behind an "I'm fine."

You know how I call myself your Proton? That silly nickname became a reminder of how much a friendship can mean. I may still overthink, get quiet, and struggle to explain myself, but I'm grateful for the person I'm becoming.

I hope you know this friendship isn't only about what you've done for me. Your happiness matters to me, your dreams matter to me, and even on your difficult days, you deserve someone who listens to you, too.

You don't have to be strong every day, Dora. You don't have to make everyone smile while forgetting your own happiness. I hope you always feel free to be yourself around me.

Maybe life will get busy. Maybe our routines will change. But I'll always be thankful that our roads crossed and that I got to call you my best friend.

No matter where life takes us, I hope we keep sharing our nonsense, cheering for each other, and irritating each other for a very long time. 😭💗

You didn't have to change my whole world to matter in it. Somehow, you made it a little softer just by being in it.

I'm forever thankful for you, Divya. For your friendship, your presence, and all the little things that make you who you are.

Always your Proton,
with love, gratitude, and a little bakwaas. ⚛️♡
